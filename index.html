Concept 01

Architectural and restrained. A cool stone gray palette with generous whitespace and Cormorant Garamond serif typography. Feels intelligent and calm — closer to a luxury wellness publication than a fitness brand.

Premium performance luxury. Warm charcoal, ivory, and bronze palette inspired by WHOOP and Oura. Strong typographic hierarchy with Cormorant Garamond, floating UI cards showing a live weekly program, and a dark metrics bar. Feels like a high-end coaching product.
